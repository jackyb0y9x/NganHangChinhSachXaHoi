SOCIAL & POLICY CREDIT GREEN TRANSITION

VBSP Organizes High-Level Dialogue on Green Microfinance & Climate-Resilient Rural Livelihoods with RABO, IFAD, and APRACA

24 October 2026 International Cooperation Dept 5 min read 1,420 views
In alignment with Vietnam’s National Green Growth Strategy and COP commitments, Vietnam Bank for Social Policies (VBSP) has announced a nationwide preferential credit expansion aimed at financing climate-resilient agriculture, rooftop solar, and circular rural supply chains across 9,964 commune transaction points.

HANOI — During a high-level policy forum held in Hanoi with representatives from Rabo Partnerships, the International Fund for Agricultural Development (IFAD), and the Asia-Pacific Rural and Agricultural Credit Association (APRACA), VBSP General Leadership underscored the essential role of preferential social credit as a direct catalytic instrument for sustainable rural development.

Prioritizing Climate-Smart Agriculture and Renewable Energy

Under the newly issued framework, VBSP branches across 63 provinces will allocate dedicated credit quotas with concessional interest rates for investments in:

  • High-efficiency drip irrigation and rainwater catchment reservoirs in drought-prone Central Highlands.
  • Subsidized household biogas biodigesters and small-scale rooftop solar equipment for livestock farms in northern midlands.
  • Salt-tolerant crop rotation and mangrove-shrimp eco-farming models across the Mekong Delta region.
  • Organic certifications and value-chain integration for One Commune One Product (OCOP) specialty goods.
“Green microfinance must not be a luxury reserved for large agro-corporations. It must reach the kitchen tables and paddy fields of the vulnerable. Through our 9,964 monthly commune transaction points, VBSP ensures that every smallholder farmer has access to affordable credit to adapt to climate challenges.”

Global Alignment with APRACA and BAAC Standards

The initiative incorporates key operational lessons from peer institutions within APRACA, particularly Thailand’s Bank for Agriculture and Agricultural Cooperatives (BAAC). Both institutions share a commitment to blending public social mandates with disciplined credit governance and community-level oversight.
